Lasagna

Home

Description

Lasagna is a delicious Italian dish made with layers of pasta, meat sauce, tomato sauce, and cheese.

It is baked in the oven until the cheese is melted and golden.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna sheets
  • Ground beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Cook the ground beef with onion and garlic.
  3. Add the tomato sauce and let it simmer.
  4. Place a layer of lasagna sheets in a baking dish.
  5. Add meat sauce and mozzarella cheese.
  6. Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used.
  7. Bake for 40 minutes.
  8. Let it cool slightly before serving.
