Couscous

Home

Description

Couscous is a North African dish made from semolina wheat that is steamed and typically served with a variety of meats, vegetables, or sauces.

Ingredients

  • Couscous
  • Water
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Tomato sauce
  • Ground beef or lamb
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Bring water to a boil in a large pot.
  2. Add the couscous and stir.
  3. Reduce heat to low and cover the pot.
  4. Let it steam for 5-10 minutes or until the water is absorbed.
  5. In a separate pan, cook the ground meat with onion and garlic.
  6. Add the tomato sauce and let it simmer.
  7. Serve the couscous topped with the meat sauce.
