Description

Pizza is a popular Italian dish consisting of a round, flattened base of leavened wheat-based dough topped with sauce, cheese, and various other ingredients.

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Pepperoni slices
  • Mushrooms
  • Green peppers
  • Onion

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 220°C.
  2. Roll out the pizza dough on a floured surface.
  3. Spread the tomato sauce evenly over the dough.
  4. sprinkle the mozzarella cheese on top.
  5. Add your desired toppings.
  6. Bake for 12-15 minutes or until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbly.
  7. Let it cool for a few minutes before slicing and serving.
